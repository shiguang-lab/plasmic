# Watermark

Library: [Ant Design v6](../index.md).

| Registered component | Registration key |
| --- | --- |
| Watermark | `plasmic-antd6-watermark` |

## Required watermark region

Use Watermark only when the design requires it, with actual content or image, rotate, font and gap. Its children retain their normal layout and interaction ownership. A watermark is presentation, not an access-control mechanism or proof that data is protected. Do not add a demo Confidential label to ordinary business content.

## Acceptance

Verify the required region and text/image, readable underlying content, pointer/keyboard usability and long/scrolling content. Inspect theme contrast and repeated placement without another surface or scroll container, then save/reopen the actual properties.
