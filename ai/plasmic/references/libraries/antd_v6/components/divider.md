# Divider

Library: [Ant Design v6](../index.md).

| Registered component | Registration key |
| --- | --- |
| Divider | `plasmic-antd6-divider` |

## Section boundary

Use Divider for a meaningful section or inline boundary. It does not own a Card surface, a heading hierarchy or arbitrary blank space. The current registration uses orientation and titlePlacement, not another major's direction/label-position props. Editable children provide the actual section text; choose semantic headings separately when required.

## Acceptance

Keep spacing on one owner and avoid a trailing divider after the last visible section. Verify horizontal/vertical placement, complete labels, conditional sections and theme contrast. Do not resize a vertical divider by adding a scrolling wrapper or insert repeated separators just to reproduce empty screenshot space.
