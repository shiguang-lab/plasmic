## Generation rules

### Text with suggested references

Mentions retains a string value; options and prefix describe its real suggestion vocabulary. Choosing a mention inserts text through the native onChange(value) contract, not an entity-object payload invented by the page. Derive any business entity lookup or storage separately from the verified requirement.

## Acceptance checks

### Acceptance

Test ordinary typing, prefix activation, keyboard selection, multiple references, empty/no-match options and disabled state. Preserve free text while options change and verify actual Form ownership when it is a field. Do not mistake a displayed suggestion for an authorized or committed business relationship.
