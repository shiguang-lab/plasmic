## Decorative border

Use BorderBeam only for a required decorative emphasis with its actual children and color contract. It does not replace a Card surface, focus indication, error state or semantic business status. Do not add it to every panel solely because it is available in the component catalog.

## Acceptance

Verify the intended boundary, theme color, readable content and absence of overlap or unexpected region sizing. Keep interaction and status meaning on their native owners. Any motion preference or advanced styling must use the actual supported implementation rather than an invented registration prop.
